How it works

One referral, start to finish

Here is how a single incident moves through the app. Tap any step to see the details.

  1. RecordA teacher logs it in a minute
  2. Apply the consequencePenalty and hours, by the book
  3. Follow throughStatus, evidence, escalation
  4. See the whole studentStanding and timeline
  5. Support with MTSSInterventions by tier
  6. Inform the familyThey see only their child
  7. Spot patternsDashboards and reports
Step 1

Record an incident in a minute

When something happens in class, the teacher writes it down right away, from a laptop or a phone.

What you can do

  • Find the student by name or ID number.
  • Pick the infraction from 61 types; the suggested consequence and points appear instantly.
  • Add the date, time, location, witnesses, description and action taken.
  • Assign the advisor or counselor who will follow up.
Who:TeachersCounselorsPrincipalAdmin
Step 2

Consequences that follow the rules

Every referral carries its penalty, and the app keeps track of whether it was served.

What you can do

  • Choose the penalty (detention, ISS, OSS, Saturday School…) and the hours or days fill in for you.
  • Mark it pending, served, partially served, rescheduled or waived.
  • Set a follow-up date and note that the parents were notified.
  • Points add up to the student's standing: Good, Warning or Probation.
Built in

The same codes as PlusPortals SIS

The app uses the discipline codes the school already uses in PlusPortals, so every entry lines up.

61infractions
17penalties
14actions
9served statuses
18locations

What you can do

  • Add, edit or hide codes (admins only).
  • Set the consequence, points and days for each infraction.
Step 3

Follow every referral through

Nothing gets lost: every incident has a status, a history and a clear next step.

What you can do

  • Move it from Open to Pending to Resolved, with a full status history.
  • Attach photos and documents as evidence.
  • Escalate to the principal in one click.
  • Export the incident as PDF or Excel.
  • Log the contact with the parents.
Step 4

See the whole student

One page gathers everything a counselor or principal needs to know.

What you can do

  • See conduct standing and MTSS tier at a glance.
  • Check family contacts, counselor, advisory and house.
  • Review totals and a timeline of every incident.
  • Print a student report.
Step 5

Support, not just consequences

MTSS (Multi-Tiered System of Supports) organizes the help each student receives.

What you can do

  • Create Tier 1, 2 or 3 interventions with a goal, monitoring plan and exit criteria.
  • Link the intervention to the incident that prompted it.
  • See reviews due in the next 30 days.
  • Export the MTSS report as PDF.
Who:CounselorsAdmin
Step 6

Families see their child, and only their child

Parents sign in from their phone and see their own child's record. Nothing else.

What they can do

  • See incidents, their status and the consequence.
  • See their child's profile and MTSS plan.
  • Access turns on only after the school verifies the link.
  • Students can see their own record, too.
Step 7

Spot patterns early

Leaders see what is happening across the school and act before it grows.

What you can do

  • See live totals: incidents, pending and resolved.
  • Filter by today, week, month, quarter or custom dates, and by grade or category.
  • Charts by category, by status and a weekly trend.
  • One-click reports by grade, by category, top 10 students and the monthly summary, to print, PDF or Excel.
Who:PrincipalCounselorsAdmin

For everyone

English y Español, on any device

The whole app switches language with one tap, and works the same on a computer or a phone.

For admins

Run it your way

Admin tools keep the information organized and secure.

  • Create users with a role, deactivate or reactivate them, and review the activity log.
  • Import the student roster from Excel.
  • Set the academic year and school settings.
  • Strong passwords, limits on sign-in attempts and automatic sign-out when idle.
Questions

What people usually ask

Can parents see other students?

No. A parent sees only their own child, and only after the school verifies the link. This is enforced on the server, not just hidden on screen.

Does it connect to PlusPortals?

It uses the same discipline codes as PlusPortals SIS, so records match. There is no automatic sync between the two systems yet.

Does it email parents?

Not yet. Staff contact the family directly and log it on the incident. Email is planned for a later version.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is designed for phones first and can be installed on the home screen.

Are the names in the video real?

No. The video and screenshots use fictional demo students.
